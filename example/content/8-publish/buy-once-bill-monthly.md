# Buy once, bill monthly: how solo agencies resell lifetime deals

You pay for your tools every month. Your clients pay you every month. The gap between those
two numbers is your margin, and a lifetime deal is the fastest way to widen it.

## What does reselling a lifetime deal mean?

It means you buy a tool once and include it in a retainer your client pays monthly. You own
the licence. The client gets the result: the report, the scheduler, the dashboard. They pay
for the outcome, not for the software.

Jordan Reyes, who runs a one-person agency with 8 retainers, put it this way: "I'm not
buying software, I'm buying margin. Every lifetime deal I resell into a retainer pays for
itself in a month and prints after that."

## How does the math work?

Redo this with your own figures. As an example:

| Line | Monthly subscription | Lifetime deal |
|---|---|---|
| What you pay | $49 a month, every month | $149 once |
| What you bill each client for the service it powers | $40 a month | $40 a month |
| Clients | 8 | 8 |
| Billed per month | $320 | $320 |
| Tool cost in month 1 | $49 | $149 |
| Tool cost in month 12 | $49 | $0 |

In this example the lifetime deal costs more in month 1 and nothing after. By month 4 it is
the cheaper line. Every month after that, the full $320 is margin.

## Which deals can you resell?

Not every deal. Check three things before you buy:

1. **The licence covers client accounts.** One purchase, every client under your login.
   Marisol Vega asked support the right question: "If I buy this once can I use it for all
   my client accounts or is it one per seat?"
2. **You can put your name on it.** A white-label option means the client sees your logo.
3. **It works on day one.** If you need a webinar to get value, your client will too.

## What stops this from working?

Buying tools you never put in a retainer. A deal you do not resell is not margin, it is a
smaller bill. Before you buy, name the client and the line on the invoice.

## Where to start

Pick the one service every client already asks you for. Find the tool behind it. Check the
licence. Then look at the [agency deals](https://acmedeals.example/agencies) for a version
you can own.
