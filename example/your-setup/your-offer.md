# Your offer

Example setup for Acme Deals, a fictional brand. The product details below were written for
this example.

## What you sell

- **Product, service, or offer:** Acme Deals, a marketplace of lifetime deals on marketing and agency software
- **One-line description:** Pay once for the tool, own it, and stop renting your software stack every month.
- **Link people should go to:** https://acmedeals.example/agencies

## Key features or components

- Lifetime deals: one payment per tool, no seats and no renewals
- Agency licences: one purchase covers every client account under your login
- White-label deals: tools you can put your own logo on and resell inside a retainer
- Reporting tools: dashboards that replace the Monday spreadsheet

## Best use cases

- A solo agency owner who wants to resell a tool into every client retainer and keep the margin
- A one-person shop that needs to look like a full team without hiring
- The only marketer at a company who needs a clean weekly number without building it by hand
