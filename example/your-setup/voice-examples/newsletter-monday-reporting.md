**Subject:** The report nobody should build by hand

**Preview:** One question to ask before Monday.

You know the Monday report. Five tabs, three exports, one number that changed and nobody knows why.

Sofia Marin is the only marketer at her company. She put it like this: "Monday is reporting hell. I'm in spreadsheets till noon and then someone asks why a number changed."

Here is the one idea for this week.

**The report is not the job. The number is.**

Your client, or your CEO, wants one number they can trust and one line on why it moved. Everything else in the report is there because it was there last week.

**Try this today**

Open last Monday's report. For each section, ask: did anyone act on this? Keep the sections with a yes. Cut the rest.

Most reports lose half their length. The half that stays is the half people read.
